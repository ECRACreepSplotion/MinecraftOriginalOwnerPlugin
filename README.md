**OwnershipTracker** is a **Minecraft Paper plugin** designed to create a persistent ownership and theft system for **valuable equipment** **(Currently available for versions 1.21.11 and 26.3)**.

Certain valuable Minecraft items are assigned a rightful owner when they first come into a player's possession. If another player possesses that item, the plugin identifies it as **stolen**, records the ownership history, and provides mechanics for **transferring ownership** or **tracking down** the rightful owner. 

The plugin has a display, modifying the item's lore to tell the player whether they **originally** own the item or if it was **stolen**. Such items that are eligible for tracking are **tools, armor pieces, and other equipment such as maces or elytras**. In version **5.0.0** the **Dragon Egg** also became eligible for tracking.
